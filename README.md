# Spartan Shields — Survive Battles with Stronger Shields

Spartan Shields 9minecraft Mod expands the single vanilla shield into a massive armory of new defensive options. It adds dozens of new standard and tower shields made from different materials so your defense can grow stronger as you gather better ores. You start with basic wood and work your way up to diamond, netherite, and even modded metals.

Tower Shields: Adds large tower shields for every material that last about a quarter longer than the standard shield of the same tier.

## What this build is

An unofficial rebuild of **ObliviousSpartan**'s original work, kept running on current Minecraft releases by 9Minecraft Studios. Not affiliated with the original author and not endorsed by them.

Most of the porting work sits in the mixin layer. Each Minecraft release moves or renames the methods this mod hooks into, so the hooks are re-pointed and re-tested against that version rather than rewritten. The behaviour underneath is upstream's and is deliberately left alone — that is the part that makes the mod feel right, and it is not ours to redesign.
